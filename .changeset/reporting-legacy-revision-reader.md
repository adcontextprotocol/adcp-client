Sellers with their own Reliable Reporting ledger tables can now cut over to the SDK ledger without dual-writing. `createReliableReportingService` (and `createPostgresReliableReportingProductionService`) accept an optional `legacyRevisionReader(request, context)`, also available as `createReportingDeliveryHandler(store, { legacyRevisionReader })`. The SDK handler always answers `get_media_buy_delivery` first; only when the SDK ledger has no such revision for the resolved account (the nondisclosing `REFERENCE_NOT_FOUND` path) does it call the reader with the same request and context. A `null`/`undefined` result yields the same `REFERENCE_NOT_FOUND`; an error the reader throws propagates. It is never consulted for an SDK-owned revision whose rows are expired, missing, corrupt or inconsistent with the committed binding, for an unusable cursor, or on capacity and other errors, so it cannot serve different content under an ID the SDK owns. The reader owns authorization and must scope by the resolved account. New `ReportingLegacyRevisionReaderV1` and `CreateReportingDeliveryHandlerOptionsV1` types. New guide: `docs/guides/REPORTING-LEDGER-CUTOVER.md`.
