Experimental AdCP 3.3 trust, grant and authorization fixes (follow-up to #3244): trust fetch rates are budgeted per (operator, own host) with a separate shared budget for `authoritative_location` hops to foreign hosts, so another operator's hop cannot exhaust a victim's budget, and a host-rate refusal is not cached as a 60-second failure; unreadable trust records raise `TrustRecordUnavailableError` and definitive delisting raises `TrustMembershipError`; a delisted bound origin (the last record read does not list it; a known delisting survives a failed refresh) is identity-established for every request, task polling included, instead of being denied everything, while an unreachable record (404/410 after a read included) is a discovery failure: spend gets retryable `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` and reads continue only within the seller's `maxOutageContinuationSeconds`; a grantor that once served trust.json and is unreachable never falls back to brand.json; a self-operated brand may grant the signer's operator directly; and a principal-resolver failure after successful verification is delivered as a standard AdCP error on tool calls (other requests are refused with that envelope) instead of a 403 with `Accept-Signature`. Patch, because the experimental 3.3 rollout's pending minor changeset covers the new exports.
