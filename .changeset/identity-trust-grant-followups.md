Experimental AdCP 3.3 trust, grant and authorization fixes (follow-up to #3244): trust fetch rates are budgeted per (operator, own host) with a separate shared budget for `authoritative_location` hops to foreign hosts, so another operator's hop cannot exhaust a victim's budget, and a host-rate refusal is not cached as a 60-second failure; unreadable trust records raise `TrustRecordUnavailableError` and definitive delisting raises `TrustMembershipError`; a delisted bound origin (including a 404/410 record, or an outage after a known delisting) falls back to identity-established (anonymous tools plus `get_principal`, no credential or resolved agent) instead of being denied everything, while an unreachable record fails spend with retryable `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` and keeps reads bound only within outage grace; a self-operated brand may grant the signer's operator directly; and a principal-resolver failure after successful verification is delivered as a standard AdCP error on tool calls (other requests are refused with that envelope) instead of a 403 with `Accept-Signature`. Patch, because the experimental 3.3 rollout's pending minor changeset covers the new exports.
