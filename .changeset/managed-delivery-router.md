Add `createReportingManagedDeliveryAdapterRouterV1`, a `ReportingManagedDeliveryAdapterV1` that dispatches to one adapter per `(pattern, transport)` so a seller can run one managed-delivery runtime across several transports (buyer GCS buckets, S3, and so on). `deliver()` and `read()` select a route from the immutable binding alone; `revoke()` selects from the pattern and transport recorded on the destination authorization, never fans out, and sends authorizations with no recorded contract to an optional `legacyDefault` route or fails with a typed `ReportingManagedDeliveryRoutingErrorV1`. Nothing unroutable reaches an adapter. Construction fails on duplicate route ids, an ambiguous `(pattern, transport)`, a route adapter without `revocationFencesDeliveryGenerations: true`, or a route with no verification profile.

`ReportingManagedDeliveryAdapterV1` gains optional hooks, all unused by a bare adapter: `probe` (the runtime calls it before adopting policy and fails startup naming the failing route id), `admitBinding` (wired into `store.installBinding` through the new optional `setBindingAdmission`, so a binding with no route or an unsupported `verification_profile` is refused at install), `admitOffering` (every advertised managed offering needs a route) and `routeIdFor`. When the adapter is a router, `runWorker()` results also carry `deliveriesByRoute`, `deliveryFailuresByRoute`, `revocationsByRoute`, `revocationFailuresByRoute` and `overdueByRoute` keyed by route id; a single-adapter runtime's result shape is unchanged. Requires the authorization `pattern`/`transport` recorded by the preceding change. No breaking changes.
