Fix the BigQuery warehouse sink never converging when a load's upload outlasts `loadTimeoutMilliseconds` (#3234). BigQuery does not stop a load when the client cancels it (the job finishes anyway) and refuses a resubmitted job ID only after the whole file is uploaded again, so every rerun re-uploaded, timed out before the 409, and looped forever. The sink now looks up the deterministic job ID (in the destination project and location) before every submission: a job that finished cleanly counts as loaded, a pending or running job is waited for within the run's deadline and never uploaded again, a job that finished with an error advances that table's attempt, and only a missing job (404) is uploaded. A run that hits the deadline rejects with the plan, job ID and cursor unchanged. Each such timeout doubles that table's deadline for later runs (capped at 6 hours) without changing the job ID, so an upload slower than the deadline eventually fits; the growth is persisted with the plan in two new columns and clears when the batch commits. The deadline-triggered cancel, its best-effort warning (`ADCP_BIGQUERY_SINK_CANCEL_FAILED`) and the assumption that a cancelled job fails are removed. `ReportingBigQueryClientV1.job().cancel` is now `@deprecated` and unused. Adds `reportingWarehouseLoadDeadlineV1`. `scripts/validate-reporting-gcp.mjs` now asserts #3234 convergence (PASS/FAIL) instead of reporting INFO.

**Re-apply `REPORTING_WAREHOUSE_SINK_MIGRATION` before running this version.** It is idempotent and adds `planned_rows_timeouts` and `planned_revisions_timeouts` to `adcp_reporting_warehouse_sinks`; a sink table without them rejects `runOnce()` with a message saying so.
