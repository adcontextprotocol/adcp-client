Add the buyer-computable coverage rule for AdCP 3.3 principal-level reporting subscriptions (experimental `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions`, #3504). `expectedSubscriptionPeriods(input)`, exported from `@adcp/sdk/reporting/consumer` and `@adcp/sdk/reporting/ledger`, is a pure function of the buyer's own records: subscription versions with their `accepted_at`, visible confirmed buys and their earlier flight values, the account's grants and timezone, the seller's offerings with their standard-dimension mapping, and the published `subscription_coverage` exceptions. It returns every owed (buy, offering, period), and `groupExpectedSubscriptionPeriodsV1` groups them into the obligations that must carry them. It implements the start rule (first boundary at or after the latest of `accepted_at`, `confirmed_at` and `authorized_at`), flight overlap as of each period's resolution, unclipped periods, the single cutover rule for new versions, deactivation, resolution changes and account timezone changes, revocation and re-grant with and without `backfill_on_regrant`, and exceptions that only remove periods.

Also exported: `createReportingPeriodCalendarV1`, the normative `period_generation` calendar for `utc`, `account_timezone`, `source_timezone` and `billing_cycle` schedules; `resolveReportingSubscriptionOfferingsV1` for `exact`, `finest` and `all` selector resolution with byte-order ties; `reportingSubscriptionScopeIncludesV1`; and `reportingConfigurationCapabilitiesV1`, which builds `configuration_tasks`, the deprecated `configuration_task` and the `subscriptions` block consistently. `createAdcpServerFromPlatform` adds `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` to `experimental_features` when the reporting capability carries a `subscriptions` block; without one the list is unchanged.

`installConfiguration` now throws a typed `ReportingConfigurationImmutableError` (`code: 'REPORTING_CONFIGURATION_IMMUTABLE'`, with `accountId`, `deliveryConfigId` and `deliveryConfigVersion`) when an existing generation is reinstalled with different content. The message is unchanged; use `isReportingConfigurationImmutableError` instead of matching it.

The cross-SDK parity table is `test/fixtures/reporting-subscriptions/coverage-cases.json`.
